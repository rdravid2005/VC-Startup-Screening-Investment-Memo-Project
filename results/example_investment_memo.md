# AtlasGrid — Preliminary Investment Memo

> **Sample framework:** First-pass educational screening based solely on the information supplied to the application.

## Executive Summary

AtlasGrid received an overall educational screening score of **80/100 (Strong)** with a **Low** final risk rating. The company is described as energy intelligence software that analyzes facility data and recommends operational efficiency actions. The screening case appears strongest in Market Opportunity and depends on validating the risks and evidence gaps described below.

## Company Overview

AtlasGrid is a **Series A** company in **Climate Tech**, primarily focused on **United States**. Its target customer is **Multi-site industrial operators**. Reported funding raised is $6.2M, and the latest valuation is $28.0M.

## Problem Being Solved

Industrial facilities struggle to identify avoidable electricity demand and carbon emissions.

The urgency, frequency, economic cost, and current customer workaround have not been independently verified.

## Product / Solution

Energy intelligence software that analyzes facility data and recommends operational efficiency actions.

The supplied differentiation thesis is: Fast deployment on existing meters plus a proprietary library of equipment-level energy signatures.

## Market Opportunity

The supplied addressable-market estimate is **$12.0B**, with estimated annual market growth of **18%**. Electrification and reporting requirements expand demand for auditable energy analytics. These figures and the underlying bottom-up assumptions require independent validation.

## Business Model

The company describes its model as **B2B SaaS**, monetized through **Annual SaaS subscription**. Reported gross margin is **78%**. Pricing, contract length, revenue concentration, implementation obligations, and expansion mechanics require further diligence.

## Traction

Reported ARR / annual revenue is **$2.4M**, annual growth is **92%**, the company serves **86 customers**, and annual customer retention is **91%**. These values are management-supplied and have not been independently verified.

## Unit Economics

Reported CAC is **$18.0K**, reported LTV is **$94.0K**, and the resulting LTV / CAC ratio is **5.2x**. Gross margin is **78%**. Cohort methodology, CAC allocation, payback period, contribution margin, and the LTV calculation remain important diligence areas.

## Competitive Landscape

The supplied competitor set is: Legacy energy-management suites; specialist consultants

The claimed differentiation is: Fast deployment on existing meters plus a proprietary library of equipment-level energy signatures.

Customer references should establish whether that differentiation is meaningful, durable, and sufficient to change purchasing behavior.

## Founder / Team Assessment

CEO previously operated industrial plants; CTO led applied machine-learning infrastructure at a public software company.

Founder-market fit, reference checks, role coverage, recruiting capacity, and ownership have not been independently assessed.

## Key Strengths

- **Market Opportunity (87/100):** Addressable market: $12.0B contributed 95/100.
- **Unit Economics (85/100):** Gross margin: 78% contributed 86/100.
- **Traction (78/100):** ARR / annual revenue: $2.4M contributed 76/100.

## Key Risks

- No metric-triggered flags were identified; unobserved risks still require diligence.

- **Management-identified context:** Long enterprise sales cycles; integrations vary by facility; savings claims require customer validation.

## Diligence Questions

1. Which critical capabilities are missing from the current team, and what is the hiring plan?
2. Which customer references can validate ROI, implementation effort, and willingness to expand?
3. What assumptions create the largest downside variance in the next 18-month operating plan?

## Preliminary Investment View

Based only on the supplied information, AtlasGrid appears **strong as a first-pass screening case**. The investment case depends on confirming the reported operating metrics, testing the differentiation with customers, and resolving the highlighted risk flags. This screen supports prioritizing the listed diligence work; it does not constitute an investment recommendation.

## Final Risk Rating

**Low risk.** This rating reflects the application’s rule-based flags and missing evidence, not a probability of success or loss.
